⚠️ Why Does Parivahan Show "Challan Not Found" For Some Vehicles?

If you have an active fine but echallan.parivahan.gov.in shows "Challan status : Challan Not Found":

  1. Parivahan requires Chassis/Engine number: When searching by "Vehicle Number" on Parivahan, it requires the last 5 characters of your chassis number. If omitted or incorrect, it automatically rejects with "Not Found".
  2. Transferred to Virtual Courts: Camera tickets older than 60–90 days in Kerala are transferred to vcourts.gov.in, which removes them from the main Parivahan portal.
  3. Recorded on State Police servers: Many AI speed camera tickets are logged on payment.keralapolice.gov.in first before central syncing.
